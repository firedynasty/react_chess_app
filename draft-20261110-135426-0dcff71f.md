## Warnings
- `chess.min.js` is chess.js 0.10: no `attackers()`/`isAttacked()`. Attackers are derived with a probe piece and legal moves.
- `index.html` has 3 `</body>` strings (two in print templates): always anchor to the last one.
- `boardSvg` is trapped in an IIFE; `getCapturedPieces` is global.
- The LLM prompt was previously fed misleading material "spikes" (half-finished exchanges) and produced wrong claims. The eval verdict must lead, and material is reported only as the net from before the mistake. Never let the prompt present the opponent's blunder as the reason a move was bad.
- `positionPvMap` is only filled by a fresh in-browser `analyzeGame()`, not Get Eval, Lichess cloud, or loaded Supabase games.
- Known unit-test gap: x-ray defenders aren't counted (12/13 in `test_detect.js`); it never flips a verdict.
- Python edit heredocs: `'\n'` becomes a literal newline, so use `'\\n'`. zsh `noclobber` needs `rm -f` then `>|`. Re-read `index.html` before editing after `sed -i`.
- Many untracked files exist (`.claude/`, `features/`, `prototypes/`, `saved_games/`); stage only `index.html`.
